The LLM Deep Dive guide has been retired

I took this guide down in November 2026. A review found errors in it that I would rather remove than patch: the layer diagram ended with softmax before logits, which is backwards; the tokeniser demonstration taught a wrong idea of how tokens are counted; and its headline figures for model sizes were unconfirmed numbers from 2023.

How language models work inside is also not where I have the most to offer. Others teach it better, and for free.

Where to learn it instead

  • 3Blue1Brown: Neural networks. A visual series that builds from a single neuron to attention and transformers.
  • Jay Alammar: The Illustrated Transformer. The transformer architecture, one diagram at a time.
  • Andrej Karpathy: Let's build GPT, from scratch, in code. For readers who learn by writing the code.

What I do cover

  • The AI Knowledge Map: the core terms and how they relate.
  • Agentic AI Runtime Architecture: how an agent runs and what bounds it.
  • Securing AI Systems and Agents: where these systems break and how to contain it.

The retired page remains in the repository history. The record of what changed on this site, and why, is on How this site is maintained.
