Thinking Skills, version 3.1 (November 2026)

- Corrected nine factual errors: the key paper on design thinking had the wrong title and DOI; a Coursera course was credited to the wrong university; Donella Meadows's highest leverage point is the power to transcend paradigms, not changing the goal; the Double Diamond dates from 2004 and its 2019 framework has four principles, not three; "thick description" was defined backwards; the six Socratic question types are Richard Paul's; and the claims about SpaceX rocket costs, an IBM "75%" result and a milkshake "7x" sales rise are removed or restated from their sources.
- Added a lab pack to each of the 36 labs: the scenario or data, a worksheet, a facilitator note on what a good answer looks like, and a "Print this lab" button. No lab now depends on material that is not on the page.
- Added a section on classical Indian reasoning: the four means of knowledge and the five-membered inference of the Nyaya tradition (with sutra numbers and the hill, smoke and fire example), and the purva paksha practice of stating the opposing view before answering it, each linked to a skill taught on the page.
- Added "Ten skills employers ask about", merged from the retired soft-skills page, with every course checked on the provider's own site, three wrong providers corrected, and the unsupported "evidence-based ranking" and statistics dropped.
- Softened claims the evidence does not support: growth mindset and metacognition are no longer said to predict success better than ability or IQ; Bloom's taxonomy is no longer presented as a strict ladder; study-technique and "five users" figures now match the papers.
- Repaired or replaced dead and wrong links (the Delphi report, the Dunlosky paper, a web literacy textbook, a TED-Ed channel, and three book links that opened the wrong book), and removed one channel that did not fit a general thinking-skills list.
- Corrected the page's own counts: lab difficulty tiers, lab hours and the progress total.
- Made the page usable from the keyboard: all 65 checklist items, every framework and lab panel, and the self-assessment dialog (Escape closes it).
- Fixed the phone layout, which was slightly wider than the screen, and raised the contrast of small grey text.
- Added the site header and footer, a licence (CC BY-NC-SA 4.0), a correction link, learning outcomes, and links to the IKS Life Skills and Cybersecurity Roadmap guides.
- Removed every em dash and several overstated words ("complete", "definitive", "authoritative") from titles and descriptions.
