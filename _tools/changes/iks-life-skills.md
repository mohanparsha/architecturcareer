IKS Life Skills Curriculum, version 1.1 (November 2026)

- Replaced the Week 9 "bystander experiment", which staged a scene without telling students, with an openly announced role-play that uses volunteers, invented character names, a debrief and an opt-out. Added "Safeguarding notes for facilitators" near the top, and a short safeguarding note inside every week.
- Removed or reworded other activities that could single out or expose a student: no naming classmates as rivals, no recording of students, private journals stay private, mock trials and disputes use invented cases, and feedback is about the role and never about a student's body, voice, family or background.
- Corrected the Week 2 story. The Sivi Jataka (no. 499) is about a king who gives his eyes; the king who offers his flesh to a hawk to save a pigeon is a different story, from the Mahabharata, and is now shown separately.
- Corrected the Week 3 source: "The Monkey and the Wedge" is in Book 2 of the Hitopadesha (and Book 1 of the Panchatantra), not Book 1 of the Hitopadesha.
- Corrected the Week 9 story of the dice game: Vidura and Vikarna do protest in the assembly, and Draupadi's question is now at the centre of the session. Removed the "loaded dice" detail, which is not in the text.
- Corrected the Week 8 source (Valmiki's sixth book is the Yuddha Kanda, not "Lanka Kanda") and the Week 7 summary (Krishna does tell Arjuna to fight, and leaves the final choice to him at 18.63).
- Added a precise citation to every story and verse (text, book or section, verse, and the translation used), a collapsed list of all sources, and the words "Traditional; source not verified" where a source could not be checked.
- Added companion sources from more traditions and with women at the centre: Gargi and Maitreyi (Brihadaranyaka Upanishad), Savitri (Mahabharata), Thirukkural couplets, a Jain text on non-violence, Amrita Devi of the Bishnoi tradition, Rani Abbakka, Ahilyabai Holkar, and articles of the Constitution of India.
- Removed 17 dead links (seven of them videos) and corrected or replaced 8 more whose labels did not match where they led (two NPTEL courses, a Gita link described as a translation it was not, two links to the 2019 UGC document labelled as 2.0). They are replaced with links to the texts themselves, all checked in November 2026.
- Added "How this relates to UGC Jeevan Kaushal 2.0": this is an independent work for Classes 9 to 12, not published or endorsed by UGC, NCERT, CBSE or any government body. The separate "Jeevan Kaushal 2.0" page has been withdrawn and merged here. Removed wording that suggested official alignment.
- Added a note on fitting the 13 periods into a school timetable, a short summary in Telugu and Hindi, and links to the printable scenario cards.
- Added the shared site header and footer, a licence (CC BY-NC-SA 4.0), keyboard access to the phase filters, and language tags for Sanskrit, Tamil, Hindi and Telugu text. Removed every em dash.
