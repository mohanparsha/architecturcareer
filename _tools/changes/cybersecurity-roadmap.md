Cybersecurity Career Roadmap, version 4.1 (November 2026)

- Corrected stale exam codes: CompTIA Security+ is now SY0-801 (launched 17 November 2026; SY0-701 stays available in English until 11 June 2027), AWS Certified Security - Specialty is SCS-C03, and Microsoft AZ-500 was retired on 31 August 2026 in favour of SC-500.
- Corrected the CrowdStrike figure: 82% of detections were malware-free in the 2026 Global Threat Report, not 79%. Replaced the claim that identity is the "#1" entry route, which the report does not make, with its figure for valid account abuse in cloud incidents (35%). All four threat numbers now link to the source.
- Added a rupee budget for the 18-month plan: what is free, what each exam costs on the vendor's own page (checked 18 November 2026), an approximate rupee figure at a stated exchange rate, and three realistic totals for a student in India.
- Corrected prices: Security+ is $307 with India selected on CompTIA's store ($439 on the US store), IAPP AIGP is $799 for non-members, and the Burp Suite Certified Practitioner exam ($99) also needs a Burp Suite Professional subscription ($499). Where a vendor does not publish a price, the page now says "check the official page" instead of quoting one.
- Removed statistics that had no source: the "1 lakh+ unfilled roles" headline, the 64% of job listings, the 144 to 1 identity ratio, the Zero Trust and purple-team percentages, the Gartner projection, the entry-level hiring and salary-uplift percentages. Every statistic that remains links to its source.
- Updated facts that had gone out of date: MITRE ATT&CK v20 (October 2026), ZAP is now "ZAP by Checkmarx" and no longer an OWASP project, Security Blue Team is now Centri, Lakera's Gandalf game has become "Gandalf: Agent Breaker", the CISM exam outline changed on 3 November 2026, and the DPDP consent-manager date has passed.
- Restored three things from the earlier version: the plain five-step incident response lifecycle (Prepare, Detect, Contain, Eradicate, Recover), XSS and IDOR named explicitly, and "get your first peer review".
- Added links from the foundation topics to this site's own guides on networking, operating systems, Secure SDLC, DevOps and DevSecOps, AI security and agentic AI.
- Added a short summary in Telugu and Hindi near the top of the page.
- Fixed "all 7 domains" (there are 8), and fixed the Resources tab running off the side of the screen on phones.
- Improved keyboard use: the arrow keys now scroll the page as normal, links inside a card no longer close it, and screen readers are told when a card is open.
- Added the shared site header and footer, with the licence (CC BY-NC-SA 4.0) and a link for reporting errors. Removed every em dash.
