Version 1.0 (November 2026): IKS Life Skills scenario cards and facilitator notes

- Added a printable pack of 67 original scenario cards for the 12-week IKS Life Skills curriculum (matched to curriculum version 1.1), so a teacher no longer has to write cards before a session.
- Added a one-page facilitator note for every week: the aim, minute-by-minute timings for one period, follow-up questions, what a thoughtful response tends to do, and how many copies to print.
- Added Tier 2 card sets where the curriculum names cards: private briefs for the playing-field negotiation (Week 1), listener cards (Week 6) and event cards (Week 10).
- Added the written briefs for the Week 9 bystander role-play, which is openly announced, uses volunteers with invented names, runs twice and has an opt-out.
- Added an opening page on printing and cutting, grouping students, ground rules for discussion and safeguarding, with the Child Helpline number 1098 checked against the Ministry of Women and Child Development page on 18 November 2026.
- Prints on 34 A4 pages in black and white, four cards to a page with cut lines; each week can also be printed on its own.
