# Known gaps (shown on the About page). One bullet each, plain words, no em dashes.

- **Cybersecurity Career Roadmap:** the India starting-salary ranges are my own indicative figures. I could not find a published survey to source them to, and the page says so.
- **Network Concepts Guide:** two of its four diagrams (encapsulation, and defence in depth) are older drawings awaiting a redraw. The defence-in-depth image contains one unsourced line, which its caption flags.
- **Secure SDLC:** the PCI DSS and SOC 2 references in the compliance map were checked against published reproductions, not the licensed originals, and the ISO/IEC 27001 control titles against the contents list of ISO/IEC 27002:2022. The KPI targets and the RACI split are examples, not benchmarks.
- **Operating Systems Unit 1:** the textbook's Chapter 2 facts were checked against a university's copy of the authors' slides, one step from the original. The learning-outcome numbers are illustrative and not taken from one university's syllabus.
- **IKS Life Skills Curriculum:** two folk stories (Birbal's khichdi, Tenali Rama and the thieves) and one Kabir couplet are marked on the page as traditional, with no verified source. The scenario cards cover every Tier 1 session, and Tier 2 only for Weeks 1, 6, 9 and 10.
- **Telugu and Hindi summaries** on the roadmap and the curriculum are short and new. If a phrase reads wrongly, tell me.
- **Testing:** every page was checked in one desktop browser at desktop and phone width, and by keyboard. None has yet been tested with a screen reader, in Safari or Firefox, or on a range of real phones.
- **Prices and versions** were checked in November 2026 and will drift. Each one sits beside a link to the official page.
